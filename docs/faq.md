# FAQ

**Do I need to speak the language of the records?** No. Claude reads Latin, German, French, Dutch, Czech and other
languages and old scripts (Kurrent, secretary hands) and gives you a translation. It can be wrong – that is why every
reading has an image and uncertain words are marked with "(?)".

**How far back can I get?** Typically to the 1700s, often the 1600s, where registers survive. Beyond that, land records,
tax lists and censuses sometimes help.

**Can I talk to Claude in my own language?** Yes. The skill and the website are in English, but Claude answers in the
language you write in. Record transcriptions stay in the original language.

**Does it work with MyHeritage, Ancestry, Geni?** You can import a GEDCOM file or a MyHeritage Family Tree Builder
backup. Other people's trees are treated as clues only and checked in the original records. Paid sites are not
scraped.

**FamilySearch?** Yes, through the browser plugin; you log in yourself. Many European registers and land records are there.

**How much does it cost?** The skill is free (MIT). You need a Claude subscription. Most archives are free; publishing
on Cloudflare/Netlify is free.

**Can I edit the data myself?** Yes, it is plain text. But it is easiest to tell Claude – it keeps the formats and runs
the checks.

**How do I update the skill?** See [Installation](../README.md#installation). After an update Claude offers to update
your project; your data and your own changes are kept.

**Something went wrong / Claude misread a record.** Tell Claude in the chat, and if it is a skill problem,
[open an issue](https://github.com/petrnetusil/genealogy-skill/issues).
