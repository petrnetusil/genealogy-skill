# Curiosities from the family tree

## 158 years in one forge
In November 1786 [Václav Novák](/person/vaclav-novak-1758/) from Ostřetín married [Kateřina Ryšavá](/person/katerina-rysava-1764/),
daughter of the Holice blacksmith [Jakub Ryšavý](/person/jakub-rysavy-1730/). Jakub had no son, so the forge in Holice no. 112 passed to his son-in-law.
From then on the Nováks held it: [Josef](/person/josef-novak-1791/), [Jan](/person/jan-novak-1824/), [Václav](/person/vaclav-novak-1858/)
and [Jan](/person/jan-novak-1890/), who worked there until his death in 1944. His son [František](/person/frantisek-novak-1921/) still trained in the forge but already worked as a locksmith.

Sources: [marriage 1786](/source/holice_marriage_vaclav_novak_katerina_rysava_1786/), [baptism of Jan 1890](/source/sample_baptism_jan_novak_1890/).

## A blacksmith family going back to the 17th century
Before Holice the Nováks were blacksmiths in neighbouring Ostřetín, in house no. 15: [Matěj](/person/matej-novak-1726/),
[Jiří](/person/jiri-novak-1694/) and the earliest known, [Jakub](/person/jakub-novak-1665/), born around 1665.
That is eleven generations of blacksmiths, hammer in hand.

Sources: [marriage of Jiří 1723](/source/ostretin_marriage_jiri_novak_alzbeta_novotna_1723/), [marriage of Matěj 1757](/source/byst_marriage_matej_novak_katerina_matejkova_1757/).

## Cholera in 1855
The blacksmith [Josef Novák](/person/josef-novak-1791/) died of cholera in Holice in 1855. His son
[Jan](/person/jan-novak-1824/), then thirty-one and unmarried, took over the forge. He married two years later.

Sources: [marriage of Jan 1857](/source/holice_marriage_jan_novak_marie_urbanova_1857/).

## The millers of Slatiňany
The Kučeras held the mill in Slatiňany no. 5 for five generations: [Václav](/person/vaclav-kucera-1773/), [Vincenc](/person/vincenc-kucera-1799/),
another [Vincenc](/person/vincenc-kucera-1831/) and [Josef](/person/josef-kucera-1866/). His daughter [Františka](/person/frantiska-kucerova-1902/)
was born in the mill and married a railwayman from Chrudim.

Sources: [marriage of Josef 1899](/source/sec_marriage_josef_kucera_anna_vavrova_1899/), [death of Františka 1985](/source/chrudim_death_frantiska_kucerova_1985/).

## An ancestor who lived to 94
[Karolína Kubátová](/person/karolina-kubatova-1852/) from Luže was born in 1852 and died in the summer of 1946.
She outlived her husband, the potter [Tomáš Vondráček](/person/tomas-vondracek-1848/), by sixteen years. She is the longest-lived ancestor in the tree.

Sources: [marriage 1873](/source/luze_marriage_tomas_vondracek_karolina_kubatova_1873/).

## A wedding at the last minute
[Václav Král](/person/vaclav-kral-1893/) and [Ludmila Hrubá](/person/ludmila-hruba-1898/) married in Holice on 8 November 1924.
Their daughter [Marie](/person/marie-kralova-1925/) was born on 2 March 1925, just 114 days after the wedding.

Sources: [death of Václav 1961](/source/holice_death_vaclav_kral_1961/), [death of Ludmila 1980](/source/holice_death_ludmila_hruba_1980/).

## The clothiers of Vysoké Mýto
At least three generations of the Hrubý family wove cloth in house no. 260 in Vysoké Mýto: [Václav](/person/vaclav-hruby-1805/),
[Václav](/person/vaclav-hruby-1841/) and [Josef](/person/josef-hruby-1871/). Josef's daughter [Ludmila](/person/ludmila-hruba-1898/) then married into Holice.

Sources: [marriage of Václav 1868](/source/chocen_marriage_vaclav_hruby_johana_fialova_1868/), [marriage of Josef 1897](/source/uhersko_marriage_josef_hruby_johana_duskova_1897/).
