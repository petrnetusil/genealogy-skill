## The blacksmith Jakub and house no. 15
The earliest Novák we know is [Jakub](/person/jakub-novak-1665/). He was born around 1665 and worked as a blacksmith in Ostřetín.
His baptism has not survived, because the Ostřetín registers only begin in 1690. But we know of him from the baptism of his son
[Jiří](/person/jiri-novak-1694/) in 1694 and from the record of his death in 1729, which gives his age as "about 64".
The forge in house no. 15 then passed to Jiří and after him to [Matěj](/person/matej-novak-1726/).

Sources: [marriage of Jiří 1723](/source/ostretin_marriage_jiri_novak_alzbeta_novotna_1723/).

## The groom who married into a forge
Matěj's son [Václav](/person/vaclav-novak-1758/) would not have inherited the forge in Ostřetín, because he had an elder brother.
So in 1786 he married [Kateřina Ryšavá](/person/katerina-rysava-1764/), the only daughter of the Holice blacksmith
[Jakub Ryšavý](/person/jakub-rysavy-1730/). Jakub died three years later and the forge in Holice no. 112 stayed with Václav.
The Nováks were there for the next 158 years.

Sources: [marriage 1786](/source/holice_marriage_vaclav_novak_katerina_rysava_1786/).

## Cholera and a new beginning
[Josef Novák](/person/josef-novak-1791/) died in 1855 in the cholera epidemic that swept through the whole Pardubice region.
His son [Jan](/person/jan-novak-1824/) took over the forge and married two years later. In the time of Jan's son [Václav](/person/vaclav-novak-1858/)
the railway came to Holice, and besides horses the forge shod cart wheels for the sugar factory.

Sources: [marriage of Jan 1857](/source/holice_marriage_jan_novak_marie_urbanova_1857/), [marriage of Václav 1884](/source/holice_marriage_vaclav_novak_barbora_mala_1884/).

## The last blacksmith
[Jan Novák](/person/jan-novak-1890/) came back from the First World War (his younger brother Václav fell on the Italian front)
and in June 1919 married [Josefa Pokorná](/person/josefa-pokorna-1895/), a farmer's daughter from Dolní Roveň. He worked at the forge until his death in 1944.
His son [František](/person/frantisek-novak-1921/) still trained in the forge, but after the war he became a locksmith in a factory.
The hearth in no. 112 went cold. But the house stayed in the family, and the anvil still stands in the yard today.

Sources: [baptism of Jan 1890](/source/sample_baptism_jan_novak_1890/), [marriage 1919](/source/sample_marriage_jan_novak_josefa_pokorna_1919/).
